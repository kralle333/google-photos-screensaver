Google Photos Screensaver

The TV's screen saver as a slideshow of a shared Google Photos album.

Album

—

In Google Photos, open the album, choose Share, then Create link, and paste the link here. Anyone with the link can see the album; the TV keeps up to the number of photos chosen above, shown in a random order, and reads the album again every six hours. More photos take longer to read the first time.

Slideshow

Each photo drifts and zooms slowly while it is shown, so nothing stays still on the panel. Dim is easier on an OLED over long hours.
